Hi, I’m

Tiffi Westcott, MS, MA

I am a psychoanalytic psychotherapist-in-training, working with patients at AthenaPsych across New York State. Currently, I’m completing my graduate training in counseling at the University of Rochester alongside my ongoing psychoanalytic formation through supervisory consultation and personal analysis.

I work with the Upstate Psychoanalytic Working Group and a cartel linked to the Lacanian Compass on Seminar I, while organizing the Upstate Psychoanalytic Society & Institute and co-leading a psychoanalytic reading group at URoc (join us!). I transcribed and edited the original manuscript of Origins of Desire & Ethics of the Drive in Lacanian Psychoanalysis by Samuel McCormick.

My clinical and theoretical research interests include community psychoanalysis, phobia, feminine sexuation, and queerness, which I have enjoyed speaking about at PATO 2025 and APA Div 39 2026, and writing about in my thesis at GCAS, advised by Todd McGowan and Ryan Engley. In my free time, I enjoy my symptom.
